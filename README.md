Nebulink

Description

Nebulink is a futuristic fusion of GitHub’s Octocat and an interstellar AI explorer.
Inspired by cyberpunk aesthetics, deep-space technology, and hand-sketched concept art, I designed this Octocat as a next-generation open-source guardian that travels across digital galaxies connecting developers, ideas, and innovation.

The artwork combines human-style sketch rendering with futuristic neon circuitry and mechanical tentacles to give it a cinematic sci-fi identity while still preserving the playful charm of the original Octocat.
